title: Hide the taskbar automatically
when: 'os = "windows"'
after: always
revision: 1
---

komorebi tiles windows over the whole work area, so the taskbar should get out of the way.

Right-click the taskbar > Taskbar settings > Taskbar behaviors > **Automatically hide the taskbar**.

No self-check: the setting is a binary blob (`StuckRects3`) that dots cannot read back.
